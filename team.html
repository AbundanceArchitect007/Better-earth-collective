FOUNDING STEWARDS

People already building it, before the paperwork catches up.

Christopher James George is, in fact and in law, the sole founder of the Better Earth Collective today. Everyone else below is a potential steward — some already part of the mission through friendship and existing collaboration, others identified from public profile and press and not yet approached. Titles, roles and figures are proposals for a founding conversation, not settled agreements, and nothing here reflects a decision by the individual named.
