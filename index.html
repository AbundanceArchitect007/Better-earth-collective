01 — STRUCTURAL DESIGN

Purpose that is structurally — not just rhetorically — protected.

A

Mission locked by constitution

A Bermuda purpose trust and a seven-seat Stewardship Council hold governance separately from ownership, so purpose cannot be sold or redirected without a supermajority reserved-matter process.

B

A doughnut-shaped scorecard

Success is measured against the UN Sustainable Development Goals' social foundation and the Stockholm Resilience Centre's ecological ceiling — before it is measured in revenue.

C

Commerce funds the mission

Nine pillars sit inside one capital-allocation policy, so profitable ventures — recovery, energy, healthcare — directly finance nonprofit and humanitarian work instead of each competing for its own funding.

03 — THE SECOND PRODUCT

The goal is for the Framework itself to be copyable — a working, audited proof that a company can be built this way and still attract capital and retain talent.

Once proven inside BEC, the Planetary Stewardship Framework is intended to be documented and offered publicly as a constitution, governance framework and accounting model other founders can adopt.
